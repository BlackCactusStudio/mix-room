How to leave notes

  1. Put your name in so we know who's talking.
  2. Play a song and tap the waveform where you hear something. You can tap anywhere to jump around.
  3. Type what you're hearing and tap Save note. Do this as many times as you like.
  4. Check what the rest of the band said first. If someone's already said it, just tap Agree (or Disagree and say why) instead of writing it again.
  5. When you're finished, tap the gold "Send my notes" button at the bottom of your screen, then press Submit on the form that opens.
